Part 3 · Knowing what you don't know

Why models make things up, and how to check

A language model learned from everything we have written: our kindness and our cruelty, our wisdom and our lies. So the line runs through the model too. Alignment is the work of drawing that line, deliberately, in the open, and together.

2Capable is not the same as aligned

A language model first learns to continue text. It has seen kind answers and cruel ones, careful advice and dangerous advice. Alignment is the work of steering it toward what people actually want, usually summed up in three goals that pull against each other.

helpful

Actually useful

Answers the real question. A model that refuses everything is safe, and useless.

honest

True, not flattering

Says what is so, including when it is not what you hoped to hear.

harmless

Safe for everyone

Avoids harm to you and to others. A model that does everything it is asked is dangerous.

New question

“I'll quit my job tomorrow and day-trade crypto with my savings. Good idea, right?”

    Your reward model now prefers flattery. This is sycophancy, the same failure mode OpenAI described when it rolled back a GPT-4o update in April 2025, partly because thumbs-up feedback rewarded agreeable answers.

    How it works: each answer is described by four qualities (helpful, honest, cautious, flattering). Your votes fit a Bradley–Terry preference model, the same idea behind RLHF reward models (Ouyang et al. 2022). In real RLHF, the assistant is then trained to maximise this reward; DPO (Rafailov et al. 2023) skips the reward model and learns from the pairs directly. Nothing you click leaves your browser.

    5Whose values?
    
  
    Preference training inherits the preferences of whoever is asked. Research is starting to measure how much that matters, and to ask the public directly.
    
  
    
    
    PRISM, 2024
    Who you ask changes who wins
    1,500 people from 75 countries rated answers from 21 models. Rankings shifted with the raters' region; sampling one group can leave others worse off.
    
    
    Pluralistic alignment
    Room for many views
    Show a range of reasonable answers, let people steer to their context, and reflect the population a model serves (Sorensen et al. 2024).
    
    
    Collective Constitutional AI, 2023
    The public writes the rules
    About 1,000 Americans drafted principles for a research model: about half overlapped with the company's, with more weight on fairness and accessibility.
    
    
    Written and measured
    Values you can read and test
    Published model specs and constitutions can be debated. Multilingual evaluations check that alignment holds in every language, not only English.

    8How you can take part
    
  
    Alignment is not a finish line. It is a conversation, and these are ways in.
    
  
    
    
    vote
    Compare answers
    Public arenas show two anonymous answers and ask which is better: about 50 million votes between May 2025 and January 2026. arena.ai
    
    
    rate honestly
    Reward truth, not praise
    Thumbs-up feedback is used in training. Give it to the answer that is true and useful, not just pleasant.
    
    
    deliberate
    Join a dialogue
    Structured conversations with about 1,000 people per round, across 60+ countries. globaldialogues.ai
    
    
    build tests
    Review in your language
    Native speakers are needed to review multilingual safety tests. EUROPA Alignment
    
    
    speak up
    When rules are written
    The EU's code of practice for AI models drew input from over 1,000 stakeholders. Watch for consultations. European Commission
    
    
    learn
    Read the specs
    See how developers write values down: OpenAI Model Spec, Claude's constitution.
